synonyms of a person.

synonyms of a person is a sound and video collage about life and the human experience. spanning two total hours, it blends together musical samples, voice memos from throughout the artist's life, spoken word of the artist's poems and journal entries, interview clips, and book excerpts. the collage takes the audience on an impassioned, meandering journey through childhood, dreams, pain, love, drugs, philosophy, and spirituality. in the artist's words — "a semi-coherent story of my life."


ideally, watch parts i and ii in a single evening. conditions — a dark room, a computer (or larger) screen, and headphones or speakers for audio.
